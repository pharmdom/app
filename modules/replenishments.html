What Can You Do Here?
Tap any card to jump straight to that feature.
Upload Files
Upload the three weekly Veeqo exports to generate this week's replenishment list. Clearance lines are stripped and SKUs are mapped automatically.
Search SKUs
Type a product name, SKU code, or EAN to instantly filter both the review queue and the actioned bucket at the same time.
View Insights
Open the weekly Insights modal to see action breakdowns, purchase totals, estimated spend, critical SKU tracking, and week-by-week history navigation.
Reset the Week
Clear all actions and notes for the current list so you can start the review session over. Rejected SKUs are preserved unless you explicitly choose to delete them too.
How It Works
Each week follows the same four-step flow.
1
Upload three Veeqo files
Click Upload and drop in the three weekly exports — File 1 is the full forecast, File 2 is the clearance forecast (used to strip clearance lines automatically), and File 3 is the full SKU catalog for code and image mapping. Click Generate List to process.
2
Work through the Review Queue
Products appear sorted by urgency — most critical first. You see one product at a time on the left. Each card shows the product image or initials avatar, urgency badge, days of stock remaining, daily sell rate, and a full quantity breakdown showing the recommended order quantity.
3
Action each SKU
Choose one of three actions — Purchased if you've placed the order, Not Available if the product can't be sourced right now (it will reappear next week), or Reject to permanently skip this SKU in all future uploads. Add a note for context. After any action the next product loads automatically.
4
Track progress in the Actioned Bucket
Everything you action moves to the right panel instantly. Filter by status tab (Purchased, Not Available, Rejected), sort by recency, days left, or name. Use the ↩ undo button on any card to send it back to the review queue. Your session auto-saves to the database — closing the tab never loses progress.
Handy Tips
Little things that make using this page much easier.
The Catalog & Offer Prices panel on each review card shows the cheapest catalog price (green) and live offer prices (blue) for that EAN. Expand it before deciding whether to purchase — it tells you who to buy from and at what cost.
Not Available clears on the next upload so the product reappears for review if still needed. Reject is permanent — use it only for products that should never be replenished again (discontinued lines). You can undo a Reject from the bucket before closing the tab.
The EAN barcode link on each card opens a Google Image Search for that barcode — a quick way to visually verify you're looking at the right product without leaving the page.
The Insights button at the top left shows a full weekly summary — action breakdown with a donut chart, estimated spend, critical SKUs, top purchased by order quantity, and week-by-week history you can navigate with the arrow buttons.
