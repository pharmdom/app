How It Works
Every product follows the same simple path from discovery to purchase.
1
Add a product
When you find something worth buying, click Add Product and fill in the details — what it is, where it's from, how much it costs, and what the profit looks like.
2
Get it approved
Click on any product row to open it. Go to the Approvals tab and a team member can approve or reject it. Products need at least one approval before they can be bought.
3
Mark it purchased
Once approved, open the product and set Purchased to Yes in the Overview tab. You'll also enter how many units were bought.
4
Leave notes along the way
The Notes tab inside each product lets you leave comments, questions, or updates — so everyone stays in the loop.
Handy Tips
Little things that make using this page much easier.
The coloured chips at the top are shortcuts — click one to instantly filter the list. Click it again to clear the filter.
Use Filters to narrow down by supplier, date, whether it's been purchased, and more — all at the same time.
Click any row in the table to open the full product detail panel on the right. Everything can be edited and managed directly from there.
When adding a product, a timer starts automatically — this helps track how long sourcing research takes on a single lead sourced.
Use the Pages menu (top left) to jump to the Sourcing Calendar — where you can see assigned hours and which suppliers to focus on each week.
