How the list is calculated
  1. Last purchase — each SKU's most recent purchase date and the stock level after it.
  2. Last order — the most recent order after that purchase; its stock level is the current stock (history_id breaks ties).
  3. Clearance SKUs are removed.
  4. Units sold = stock after purchase − stock after last order.
  5. Days = last purchase date → today.
  6. Daily rate = units sold ÷ days.
  7. Days of stock left = current stock ÷ daily rate.
  8. 25-day requirement = daily rate × 25, rounded up.
  9. Incoming = total open PO quantity per SKU.
  10. Order quantity = 25-day requirement − current stock − incoming, never below 0.
  11. Flags: out of stock, stock drop ≠ order quantities, no sales since purchase, incoming PO (and overdue PO).

Lines that are out of stock are recorded and brought back for a check in each of the next 4 weekly cycles.
