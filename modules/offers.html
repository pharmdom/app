How It Works
Offers flow through a simple pipeline from upload to sourcing.
1
Upload a supplier pricelist
Click Upload and drop in a CSV, XLSX, or PDF. Select the header row, map the Product Name column (and optionally EAN and cost), pick the supplier, then confirm. All rows land on the board instantly.
2
Review offers on the Kanban board
Each supplier gets its own column. Cards show the product name, EAN, cost, and — when available — how the price compares to what's already in the catalog. Green badges mean cheaper than catalog; red means more expensive.
3
Approve or reject each offer
Click the price pill on any card to open the detail panel. Use the Approve button to fill in sourcing details and move it to the Sourcing page, or Reject to log a reason and move it to the rejected list. You can also use the quick action buttons directly on the card.
4
Approved offers become sourcing opportunities
When you approve an offer, a form opens to complete the sourcing details (ASIN, financials, packing, competition). On save, the offer is removed from this board and a new sourcing entry is created automatically.
Handy Tips
Little things that make using this page much easier.
Switch between Kanban, Card, and Table views using the toggle in the toolbar. Kanban groups offers by supplier; Card view shows everything in a flat grid; Table is the most compact for scanning large batches.
Use Merge to combine two supplier columns that represent the same supplier with slightly different names. Select two or more — the first selected name is used as the target.
The Cheapest for EAN smart filter highlights only the best-priced offer for each EAN across all suppliers — ideal when the same product appears from multiple sources.
When a detail drawer is open in Kanban view, use the ← → arrow keys to navigate between offers from the same supplier — no need to close and reopen.
