What Can You Do Here?
Tap any card to jump straight to that feature.
Search Products
Type a product name, ASIN, or supplier to instantly filter the list down to matching products.
View Approved Only
Click the green Approved chip at the top to filter down to only the products that have passed all FBA criteria.
View Rejected Only
Click the red Rejected chip to see which products didn't pass — you can still open them and update the FBA Form if conditions change.
Assess a Product
Click any card to open the detail panel, then go to the FBA Form tab to fill in the assessment fields and get an instant pass/fail result.
How It Works
The FBA assessment follows a fixed set of criteria — fill in the form and the result is calculated automatically.
1
Products appear here automatically
Any product in the Listing & Veeqo page that has been flagged as an FBA opportunity shows up here. You don't add them manually — the flag is set automatically when the right conditions are met.
2
Open a product and fill in the FBA Form
Click any card to open the detail drawer, then switch to the FBA Form tab. Fill in the assessment fields — DG status, FBA fees, competition, parcel weight, standard pack size, ASIN sales, and profit ROI.
3
The result is calculated live
As you fill in the fields, the system walks you through each condition step by step. Once all required fields are filled, a green Approved or red Rejected result appears at the bottom — along with the specific reason if it fails.
4
Everything saves automatically
There's no Save button on the FBA Form — every field saves 300ms after you stop typing. The "Saved ✓" indicator in the footer confirms it. The assessment result is also saved, so it persists across sessions.
Handy Tips
Little things that make using this page much easier.
Use the ← → arrow keys to navigate between products while the detail drawer is open — no need to close and reopen it each time.
Switch between Grid and List view using the toggle buttons in the top right of the toolbar — list view is more compact when you have a lot of products to scan through.
The DG (Dangerous Goods) field changes the profit threshold — DG products need ≥45% ROI, non-DG products need ≥35%. Make sure this is set correctly before reading the result.
The FBA Form reveals fields progressively — each field only appears once the previous condition is met. This guides you through the assessment in the correct order and prevents confusion.
