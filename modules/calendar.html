What Can You Do Here?
Tap any card to jump straight to that feature on the page.
Schedule a Block
Assign an agent to visit a supplier on a specific day with a set number of hours. You can also add categories and must-do notes.
Set the Weekly Budget
Set how much is available to spend on sourcing this week. The budget bar at the top tracks how much has been used and what's left.
View Insights
Open the Insights panel to see hours by agent and supplier, a daily load heatmap, catalog approval rates, and which suppliers haven't been visited recently.
How It Works
The calendar follows a simple weekly rhythm — plan, track, complete.
1
Set the week's budget
Click Budget in the top right to enter how much is available for sourcing this week.
2
Schedule sourcing blocks
Click Add Block or hover over any day cell and click + Add. Choose the agent, date, supplier, how many hours to allocate, and any categories or notes for sourcing
3
Track progress during the week
Click any block to open its detail panel. Use the status button in the bottom right to move it from Not started → In progress → Done. You can add a completion note when marking it done.
4
Review with Insights
Click Insights at the end of the week or at any time to review how hours were distributed across agents and suppliers, check catalog approval rates, and identify suppliers that haven't been visited recently.
Handy Tips
Little things that make this page much easier to use.
The blue hours badge on each agent's photo updates live as you add or remove blocks — use it to spot if someone is overloaded or has capacity to spare.
You can drag any sourcing block from one cell to another to reschedule it. Drop it on a different day or a different agent's row and it saves automatically.
The budget bar turns amber when 70% is used and red at 90% — a heads-up before you overspend.
Use the left and right arrows to navigate between weeks. Each week has its own blocks, budget, and replenishments — everything updates when you switch.
The Agents button lets you hide agents from the calendar view — useful when you only want to focus on one or two people's schedule for the week.
