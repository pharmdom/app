What Can You Do Here?
Tap any card to jump straight to that feature.
Add a Shift
Open the shift modal to schedule a task or break for any employee. Select the employee, task type, date, start time, and end time. Conflicts are detected automatically.
Manage Team
Show or hide individual employees from the rota grid. Useful for focusing on a single department without deleting anyone's data.
Copy Schedule
Duplicate an entire day, week, or two-week period to a new date. Preview which shifts will be copied and remove any you don't want before confirming.
Mark Holiday
Add a shift and pick the "Holiday" task — same as scheduling any other task. Its hours are automatically deducted from that employee's holiday balance for the year.
How It Works
The rota grid shows every active employee alongside the selected time period.
1
Navigate to the right period
Use the ◀ ▶ arrows to move forward or back. Click the date label to open a calendar picker and jump to any date directly. Use the view selector (Day / Week / 2 Weeks) to control how much of the schedule is visible at once.
2
Add shifts to the grid
Click Add Shift or click any empty cell in the grid to open the shift modal. Select the employee, choose Task or Break, pick the task type, date, and use the time picker to set start and end times. The system warns you of any overlapping shifts before saving.
3
Edit or delete existing shifts
Click any shift block (or week pill in week view) to open it for editing. The small ✕ trash button on each shift deletes it after a confirmation prompt. Changes saved instantly and revert automatically if a save fails.
4
View an employee's full schedule
Click any employee's name or avatar in the left column to open their detail drawer. It shows all their shifts for the current period, grouped by day with total hours. Each shift links back to the edit modal.
5
Copy a period forward
Click Copy to open the copy modal. Choose a source date, the span to copy (day / week / 2 weeks), and the destination start date. Preview all shifts that will be duplicated, remove any you don't want, then confirm. Shifts are saved in parallel.
Handy Tips
Little things that make using the rota much easier.
In Day view, each column is a 15-minute slot — click any slot to pre-fill the start time in the shift modal. In Week or 2 Weeks view, clicking an empty cell opens the modal with that employee and date pre-selected.
When a new shift overlaps an existing one, a conflict banner appears in the modal. Click Replace to overwrite the conflicting shift, or adjust the times to avoid it.
Use Team to hide employees you're not scheduling right now. Hidden employees disappear from the grid but their shifts are never deleted — just re-show them to bring everything back.
The Copy feature is the fastest way to repeat a standard week. Set up one good week, then copy it forward as many times as needed — removing any exceptions in the preview before confirming.
