Payroll
Welcome to Payroll
Paycheck is the payroll tracking hub. It pulls shift data directly from the Rota, calculates each employee's earnings based on their hourly rate, and lets you mark weeks as paid or pending — all in one place.

What Can You Do Here?
Tap any card to jump straight to that feature.
Mark a Week as Paid
Click any employee row to open their drawer, then use the Paid button on any pending week. The status updates instantly and syncs instantly.
Add a Bonus
Inside an employee's drawer, click the Bonus button on any unpaid week to add a one-off bonus amount with an optional note. Bonuses appear in payslips automatically.
Filter by Date Range
Use the date filter bar to narrow down to This Week, This Month, Last Month, Last 3 Months, or a custom date range. All totals and employee rows update instantly.
Download Payslip
Employees see a Payment History tab with a payslip downloader. Select a week or month and download a branded PDF payslip with full earnings breakdown.
How It Works
Paycheck reads directly from the Rota — no manual data entry needed.
1
Data comes from the Rota
Every shift saved in the Rota carries the employee's hourly rate at the time it was created. Paycheck multiplies those hours by that rate to calculate each day's earnings. Breaks are automatically excluded from pay calculations.
2
Shifts are grouped into weeks
All shifts from Monday to Sunday are grouped into a single week block per employee. Each week shows the total hours, hourly rate, and total pay. The weekly view makes it easy to pay staff in one action rather than shift by shift.
3
Mark weeks as paid
Click an employee row to open their detail drawer. Each week has a Paid button — clicking it updates all shifts in that week to paid status instantly. You can undo this at any time with the Undo button.
4
Add bonuses to any week
For weeks that haven't been paid yet, use the Bonus button to add a one-off payment on top of the regular hours. Enter the amount and an optional note (e.g. "Performance bonus"). The bonus is stored against the first shift of that week and shows up in payslips.
5
Employee self-service view
Employees logging in see only their own pay records — no access to other staff data. They can browse This Month, All Records, or Payment History, and download PDF payslips for any paid week or month directly from the page.
Handy Tips
Things that make Paycheck easier to use day-to-day.
The hourly rate shown for each week is the snapshot rate at the time the shift was created. If an employee's rate changes, older shifts keep the original rate — historical pay is always accurate.
The orange chip at the top filters to employees with at least one unpaid week. The green chip filters to fully paid employees. Click either chip again to clear the filter.
The summary banner always reflects the current date filter and status filter. If you're looking at "Last Month" with "Has Pending" active, the totals show only pending amounts within that period.
Payslip PDFs are only available for paid weeks. If a week shows as pending, mark it as paid first, then download the payslip from the Payment History tab.

What Can You Do Here?
Tap any card to jump straight to that feature.
Add a Staff Member
Create a new team account with a name, email, role, hourly rate, and module access. A welcome email is sent automatically.
Search the Team
Type a name or email to instantly filter the staff cards. Combine with the role chips above for more specific filtering.
View Suspended Staff
Click the red Suspended chip to see all accounts currently suspended. Open any one to reinstate or remove them.
View a Staff Profile
Click any card to open a full profile — details, role, tenure, and all module access. From there you can edit or remove the account.
How It Works
Adding and managing a staff member follows a simple flow.
1
Add a staff member
Click Add Staff at the top right. Fill in their name, email (must be @pharmdomestic.com), password, role, hourly rate, and which modules they can access. Click Add Staff Member — a welcome email is sent automatically.
2
Module access controls what they see
Each staff member only sees the modules they've been given access to. The module groups in the form (Business Analysis, Products, Procurement, Warehouse, Others) let you toggle individual pages on or off. Use Select all to grant an entire group at once.
3
Edit a staff member
Click any staff card to open their profile, then click Edit in the footer. You can update any field — name, role, status, hourly rate, or module access. An email is sent automatically if their role or status changes.
4
Remove a staff member
Open their profile and click Remove. You'll see a confirmation — once confirmed, their account and login are permanently deleted, and an offboarding email is sent.
Handy Tips
Little things that make using this page much easier.
The role chips at the top (Admin, Digital Ops, Warehouse, Suspended) are quick filters — click one to see only that group. Click it again to clear the filter.
Each staff card shows a tenure badge — how long they've been with the team. Green = new, blue = mid-term, amber = senior.
Only @pharmdomestic.com email addresses are accepted. The email field validates the domain in real time and shows a red error if you try a different domain.
When editing, leave the password field blank to keep their existing password. Only fill it in if you actually need to reset it.
