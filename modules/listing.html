What Can You Do Here?
Tap any card to jump straight to that feature.
Search Products
Type a product name or ASIN to instantly filter the board down to matching cards.
Filter the Board
Use Filters to narrow by warehouse status, arrival, merged, or bundled — combine as many as you need.
View Fully Listed
Click the purple "Fully Listed" chip at the top to open a full table of all products that have completed warehouse and delivery steps.
Open a Product
Click any card on the board to open its full detail panel — edit fields, update checklist items, and move it to the next stage.
How It Works
Every product follows a fixed path across three columns before it's fully listed.
1
Needs Listing
A product starts here when it's been purchased but hasn't been set up yet. Open it and go to the Platforms tab to activate Amazon advertising campaigns and — if quantity exceeds sales — eBay and TikTok listings. Once all platforms are active, click Start Veeqo & Marketing to move it forward.
2
Veeqo & Marketing
Here you complete the Veeqo checklist — title, weight, dimensions, merged, bundled — and the Marketing checklist — multi-buy offers, B2B pricing, ads, and seller toolkit. A product moves to the next column automatically once all checklist items are ticked.
3
Awaiting Delivery
All listings and checklists are complete. When the physical stock arrives at the warehouse, open the product and tick Warehouse Notified and Delivery Received in the Overview tab. Once both are done the product moves to Fully Listed.
4
Fully Listed
The product has completed all steps. It no longer appears on the Kanban board but shows up in the Fully Listed table accessible from the purple chip at the top of the page.
Handy Tips
Little things that make using this page much easier.
You can drag any card between columns to manually move it to a different stage. Just grab and drop — the change saves automatically.
The eBay and TikTok listing prices in the KPI bar are calculated automatically from the unit cost and pack size — they update live as you type.
eBay and TikTok are auto-managed — they only appear as required when the quantity purchased exceeds the sales figure. If qty ≤ sales, they show as N/a automatically.
You can't tick Warehouse Notified or Delivery Received until the full Veeqo checklist is complete — this prevents products skipping the setup steps.
