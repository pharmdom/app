What Can You Do Here?
Tap any card to jump straight to that feature.
New Invoice
Create a blank invoice with the next sequential INV-XXXX number pre-assigned. Fill in the client details, add line items, and save when ready.
Search Invoices
Search by invoice number, client name, or date to quickly find an existing invoice in the left panel list.
Download PDF
Generate and download the currently selected invoice as a print-ready PDF. The PDF matches the on-screen layout exactly, including the company logo.
Email Invoice
Send the current invoice by email with a PDF attachment. Pre-fills the subject and a professional message body — just add the recipient and send.
How It Works
Invoices are created, edited, and sent entirely within this page.
1
Create or select an invoice
Click New Invoice to start a blank invoice, or click any existing invoice in the left panel to open it. The right panel shows the editable invoice immediately.
2
Fill in the invoice details
All fields are editable inline — click directly on the client name, address fields, or date to edit them. Add line items with Add Line Item, entering a description, quantity, and the Line Item Price (£). Pre-VAT and VAT amounts are calculated automatically based on the VAT rate you select per line.
3
Save the invoice
Click Save in the top right of the preview panel. New invoices are inserted into the database; existing ones are updated. The left panel list refreshes automatically and the total value chip in the header updates.
4
Download or send
Use the Share dropdown to either download a PDF or open the email modal. The email modal pre-fills the subject, a professional message body with payment details, and attaches the invoice as a PDF automatically before sending.
Handy Tips
Little things that make using this page much easier.
Enter the Line Item Price (£) — the highlighted amber field — as the final price the client pays. Pre-VAT and VAT are back-calculated automatically from the VAT rate you choose per line (0%, 5%, or 20%).
Invoice numbers are assigned automatically in sequence (INV-0001, INV-0002…). You never need to manually number an invoice — the system picks up from the highest existing number.
The Delete button requires a two-step confirmation dialog before permanently removing an invoice — so accidental clicks are safe.
The email modal sends the invoice with the PDF already attached — you don't need to download and attach it manually. Just enter the recipient email and click Send.
